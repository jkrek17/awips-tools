ERA5 hurricane-force-equivalent criterion: cross-period transfer test
========================================================================
The criterion is a PROXY: it reproduces what OPC's archive called hurricane
force from ERA5 cyclone features. It cannot be validated before 2001. These
results are a necessary condition for backward use, not a sufficient one.

Seasons fetched: [1979, 1980, 1981, 1982, 1983, 1984, 1985, 1986, 1987, 1988, 1989, 1990, 1991, 1992, 1993, 1994, 1995, 1996, 1997, 1998, 1999, 2000, 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004, 2005, 2006, 2007, 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025]   (season N = 1 Sep N to 31 May N+1)
Early window used: (2004, 2005) (both basins; archive RECORD_START = 2004)
Late window used : (2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025)
Sensitivity S2   : early window plus Pacific (2002, 2003)
NOT the nominal 2001-2005: the archive is short-counted before 2004-05 (Pacific
begins Feb 2002, Atlantic Sep 2003), so uncovered HF cyclones would be labelled
negative and the label noise would differ by period. Windows are 2 and 5 seasons.

10. HSS, POD, FAR AT THE COUNT-MATCHED CUT
   Unit: a 6-hourly cyclone-moment (fit population, pos/neg). Yes-forecast = p >= cut, where the cut is
   the probability at which the number of flagged moments EQUALS the archive's HF-moment count in the
   fit window (so flagged = obs there, and POD = 1 - FAR by construction in-sample). POD = hits/(hits+miss);
   FAR = false alarms / flagged (a ratio, not a rate); HSS = 2(ad-bc)/[(a+c)(c+d)+(a+b)(b+d)], 0 = no skill
   over chance, 1 = perfect; CSI = hits/(hits+FA+miss). 'cut ~kt' = the gust the cut equals: exact for M0
   (a gust-only model IS a gust threshold); for M1 the gust at which M1 reaches the cut with its other
   features at the median of the training positives. Transfer rows apply the TRAINING window's cut to the
   other window, so flagged vs obs there is the count error a backward extension would inherit.
   Intervals in brackets: 95% on HSS, resampling evaluation tracks, fit held fixed (400 reps).
