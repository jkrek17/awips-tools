ERA5 hurricane-force-equivalent probability series (hf_probability.csv.gz)
========================================================================
THIS SERIES IS A PROXY. It gives, per ERA5 cyclone and 6-hourly step, the probability that OPC's
archive would have called the cyclone hurricane force, from ERA5 cyclone features. It is NOT an
extended hurricane-force record, it cannot be validated before the archive's RECORD_START (2004),
and its seasonal sums are model expectations in cyclone-moments, not observed event counts.

Seasons scored: 1979..2025 (13: 1979, 1980, 1998, 1999, 2002, 2003, 2004, 2005, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025)
Fit seasons   : full (2004, 2005, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025) | early (2004, 2005) | late (2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025)
Pre-2001 seasons in this run: 4 (of 22: 1979-2000).

Stationarity flags at the 95% level: none raised.
Borderline (not significant at 95% given the small n, but above |t|=2): sum p_full TRENDS across the pre-2001 seasons available - the ERA5 gate says the fields do not (slope +55.21 /decade, t=4.03, critical 4.30 at n=4)
Borderline (not significant at 95% given the small n, but above |t|=2): series (all rows) minus archive pos moments TRENDS in the overlap (slope +19.73 /decade, t=2.46, critical 2.57 at n=7)
Power caveat: 4 pre-2001 season(s) cached; the overlap is two clusters 15 years apart. Read section 5 before using any trend.
Overlap, mean per season 2004-2005 -> 2021-2025: series sum (all rows) +33%, fitted-population sum +21%, archive pos moments +21%, archive events +25%.

   sum(p_full) trend, or step between eras, beyond what the archive's own counts do? If it did, the criterion
   would be importing a trend the fields do not have. Regressions are OLS on season (per decade).
