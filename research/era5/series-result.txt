ERA5 hurricane-force-equivalent probability series (hf_probability.csv.gz)
========================================================================
THIS SERIES IS A PROXY. It gives, per ERA5 cyclone and 6-hourly step, the probability that OPC's
archive would have called the cyclone hurricane force, from ERA5 cyclone features. It is NOT an
extended hurricane-force record, it cannot be validated before the archive's RECORD_START (2004),
and its seasonal sums are model expectations in cyclone-moments, not observed event counts.

Seasons scored: 1979..2025 (47: 1979, 1980, 1981, 1982, 1983, 1984, 1985, 1986, 1987, 1988, 1989, 1990, 1991, 1992, 1993, 1994, 1995, 1996, 1997, 1998, 1999, 2000, 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004, 2005, 2006, 2007, 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025)
Fit seasons   : full (2004, 2005, 2006, 2007, 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025) | early (2004, 2005) | late (2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025)
Pre-2001 seasons in this run: 22 (of 22: 1979-2000).

*** STATIONARITY FLAG(S): ***
*** sum p_full TRENDS across the pre-2001 seasons available - the ERA5 gate says the fields do not (slope +35.39 /decade, t=3.19 > 2.09 critical, n=22)
