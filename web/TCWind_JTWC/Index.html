Time history: Vmax (black) and Rmax (gray dashed, right axis) vs forecast hour. Colored bands are each threshold's quadrant min-max range (the wider the band, the more asymmetric the bulletin's own radii are) with the quadrant mean as a line. Vertical line marks the currently selected time.
Azimuthal profile at the selected time: modeled wind speed vs radius along each quadrant bearing. Dots are the bulletin's own reported radii - a dot sitting on its line is a good fit; off the line means the interpolation is straying from what JTWC reported.
Verification: tool's predicted Rmax vs JTWC's own post-season best-track RMW, every usable WestPac record 2001-2024. Diagonal line is perfect agreement; points below it are underestimates. Colored by intensity category. Picking a storm above shows its actual track/radii/RMW (magenta dashed ring) on the map, next to the tool's own modeled output, exactly like a live bulletin.
