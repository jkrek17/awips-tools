Extratropical transition test lab

Does the GTCM fit need help during transition — and if so, which kind?
Live tool Archive Findings

Transition expands and flattens a cyclone's wind field. The GTCM already has the parameters to say that — the core radius rm and the size exponents x1/x2 — so the question is not whether the expansion can be expressed but whether the unmodified fit is already getting it from the bulletin's own radii. This page scores that first, then scores two candidate additions against it.

What is being measured. Every archived best-track record that reports at least one wind radius, for the storms that reach a transition stage, is fitted and compared with what was reported. Two numbers per variant: the weighted wind error the GTCM's own least squares minimises (Users Guide eq. 7), and the threshold-radius error it does not — how far the modelled 34/50/64 kt radius sits from the reported one on each quadrant bisector. The second is the one that answers “is the field too tight during transition”.
