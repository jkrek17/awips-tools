Sources: NESDIS Metop-B ASCAT, full swaths over the open ocean, posted about 1 h after the pass (NOAA AOML, through NOAA ERDDAP); KNMI Metop-C ASCAT 25 km, full swaths over the open ocean, listed by NASA PO.DAAC about 2.5 to 4 h after the pass; NOAA STAR, posted about 3 to 4 h after the pass (OSCAT-3, and ASCAT, which is a coastal product there); and Remote Sensing Systems (RSS) ASCAT, Metop-B and Metop-C, full swaths over the open ocean, posted about 2 to 5 days after the pass and replacing NESDIS's Metop-B and KNMI's Metop-C where they overlap. Winds are 10 m as served (likely equivalent-neutral), averaged to 0.25° cells; rain-flagged cells are faded and left out of every score. Best Wind and the warning lines come from the marine wind page; in the mosaic they cover the whole basin at 00Z or 12Z, while the scores count only the cells a satellite saw, each against the forecast hour nearest its own pass; see verification for the longer record.
