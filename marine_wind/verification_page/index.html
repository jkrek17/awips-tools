How it's scored

Every day the forecasts that were valid two days earlier are rebuilt with the wind page's own code, from the same model runs on the AWS open-data mirrors, and compared with what was observed. North Atlantic: valid 12 UTC, from the 12 UTC runs. North Pacific: valid 00 UTC, from the 00 UTC runs. Sustained 10 m wind, open ice-free water in the OPC chart area. Scores go back to March 2025, when AWS starts holding all of the models.

What is scored

  • Core Lifted: the wind page's main wind, the blend + 5 kt where the core likelihood is 35% or more (storm cores kept).
  • Blend, no core lift: GFS, ECMWF and AIFS weighted at each point by member support, + min(k × member spread, 5 kt).
  • Deterministic blend: the GFE candidate that needs no members (9 lagged runs). Scored only when all 9 runs are there.
  • 3-model average: plain average of GFS, ECMWF and AIFS.
  • GFS, ECMWF, AIFS: each model's own wind.
  • GEFS, ENS and AIFS ENS means: the speed of the members' average wind, as AWIPS shows an ensemble mean. Averaging smooths the peaks away, so these run low in storms. The AIFS ensemble is on AWS from August 2025.

Truth

  • Analysis: the average of the GDAS and ECMWF analyses, every water cell. GDAS and IFS are each alone. Analyses smooth the strongest winds, so they see fewer storm-force cells than ASCAT.
  • ASCAT: Metop-B and C within 90 minutes of the valid time, rain-flagged cells dropped, turned from equivalent-neutral into real 10 m wind with the analysed air-sea temperature difference. At 12 UTC the swaths miss most of the Pacific, which is one reason the Pacific is scored at 00 UTC.
  • Buoys: moored buoys raised to 10 m. Ships + buoys adds ships, scaled by 0.88 onto the buoy basis. Both come from the GDAS marine monitoring file (first guess + observed departure).

Scores

  • Bias: average forecast minus truth, all points (+ means too strong).
  • Miss at 25+ kt: average size of the error where the forecast or the truth is 25 kt or more.
  • Caught: of the cells or obs at gale (34), storm (48) or hurricane force (64), the share the forecast had at that force. False alarms: of the cells the forecast put at that force, the share that weren't. Area: forecast area ÷ observed area (1.0 = right size).
  • Storm peaks: 5° × 5° boxes with 150+ scored cells. Caught = boxes where storm force was seen anywhere that also had it forecast anywhere. This is the "a few pixels of storm force means a storm warning" view, so it doesn't punish a core placed 50 km off. Peak off by: the box's strongest forecast wind minus its strongest observed wind, over boxes where either reaches gale.
  • Chances: Brier skill against always forecasting how often it happened in the period (1 perfect, 0 no better). The chart groups the forecasts in 10% steps.
  • Storm layers: hazard lines cell by cell (inside = share of obs at the force inside the line; verified = share of outlined cells at the force; area = outlined ÷ observed) and the ensemble low track against analysed lows.

Read with care

  • The core likelihood was trained on North Atlantic ASCAT from winter 2025-26 (Nov to Mar), and the blend was tuned on winter 2025-26 and fall 2026 cases. Scores for Core Lifted and the blend in those periods flatter them.
  • A few storm-force cases make a percentage. Check the counts in the column headings before reading much into storm and hurricane force.
