Reading the tables

  • Latest 4 weeks: the mean of the newest four ISO weeks of scores, each week weighted by its count n (cases, lows or events, as the score defines it). A basin choice above applies to the table and the trend lines.
  • Original interval: the 95% range the study itself found for that score, from resampling whole weeks. It is for both basins pooled, whatever the basin choice.
  • Drift: ok when the recent mean sits inside the original interval, drift when it falls outside, not enough weeks when too few weeks have been scored yet. Drift is a prompt to look, not a verdict.
  • Method names: IFS wind + N kt warns where the IFS 10 m wind plus N kt reaches the level (gale 34 kt, storm 48 kt). Ensemble share ≥ P% warns where at least P% of the GEFS, ENS and AIFS ENS members reach it.
  • Weeks run Monday to Sunday and are named by the run (cycle) date. The independent sample is weeks, not single storms or cases.
