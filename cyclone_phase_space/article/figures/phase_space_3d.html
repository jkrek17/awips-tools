Cyclone phase space: the seven HCPSclass cells and the synthetic life cycle

Each axis is one of Hart's three phase-space parameters: the lower thermal wind -VTL, the thermal asymmetry B and the upper thermal wind -VTU, all in meters. The planes B = 10 m, -VTL = 0 and -VTU = 0 divide the space into the seven colored HCPSclass cells; class 6 (cold below, warm above) spans both sides of B = 10 m. The two paths are the synthetic 168 h life cycle of Figure 13 evaluated by Hart's storm-centered method (gray circles) and by the gridded method (red squares), each shaded light to dark from 0 h to 168 h; the x marks the gridded onset (42 h) and completion (114 h).
